Valentines Day Icon Set

Year: 2025

Project Overview

Cute valentines day icons designed not only for digital purposes, such as social media, embelishments, etc., but also for print material, such as stickers.

Design Process

I first started by thinking about what theme I wanted to make my icons. I chose to make them Valentines Day related. I then needed to decide what objects I wanted. In order to figure this out, I made sketches of multiple different items and chose my favorite ones, after that, I built them out in Illustrator.
